The problems

parser: day and month swap. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” It reaches every tenant through the app, email and both booking forms.

The kb files the booking-v3 form reports apart, as hint. They are the same bug. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY, the order the parser already reads, so a person who follows the hint gets the right date. A swapped date through that form means the person typed the day first, as their country does, and the parser read it month first. One fix closes both.

one-day: a day off, not a swap. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” These are time zone moves. The desk already read them this way (desk-notes.json) but still counted them under parser.

How to read the counts

  • One id, one report: the export repeats some reports, so the table counts unique ids.
  • The tenant decides the problem, not intake’s tag. Intake’s tag is a guess from the browser, and its hint tag is not a problem of its own (see the table below).
  • Every number on this page is computed at render time from the export. None is typed.
