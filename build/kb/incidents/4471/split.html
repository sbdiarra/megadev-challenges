The problems

parser: day and month swap. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” It reaches every tenant through the app, email and the booking-v2 form. This is the fix that is queued.

hint: the form tells people to type it wrong. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the date day first. People do what the hint says, and the date swaps. Only the tenants on booking-v3, only through the form. The symptom is the same as parser, so the subject rule merged it. The fix is not the same, and it had no ticket of its own: the desk’s table below already marks the row “not this ticket”.

The one-day moves. The desk read a few parser reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). Their tenant files them under parser, so they are counted there, once, and footnoted under the table.
